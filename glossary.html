Glossary

Medical disclaimer: This private guide is general education compiled from published sources and the public website of Dr. Rovner's practice. It is not medical advice and does not replace the instructions Sarah gets from her surgical team. If anything here differs from what they say, follow them. For trouble breathing or rapidly increasing neck swelling, call 911 (see red-flag card).

Plain-language definitions of terms used in this guide and in surgeons' notes.

ACDF (anterior cervical discectomy and fusion)
Removing a disc through the front of the neck and fusing the two vertebrae with bone graft or a cage, usually with a plate and screws. It is the most commonly performed operation for cervical radiculopathy [27].
ADR / CDA / CTDR / TDR
Artificial disc replacement, cervical disc arthroplasty, cervical total disc replacement. These all mean the same operation: replacing a disc with a moving implant [25].
Adjacent segment degeneration / disease (ASD)
Wear at the levels next to a treated level. "Degeneration" usually means X-ray changes; "disease" means symptoms. Fusion may speed it up by removing motion [54, 29].
Annulus fibrosus / nucleus pulposus
The tough outer ring and the soft jelly-like center of a disc [26].
Composite (overall) success
The FDA trial endpoint combining enough NDI improvement, no neurological worsening, no secondary surgery at the treated level, and no major complication [11, 47].
CPT code
Billing code. 22856 is one-level cervical disc arthroplasty, and 22858 is the add-on code for a second level [22].
Dermatome / myotome
The skin area and the muscle group mainly supplied by one nerve root [32].
DEXA T-score
A bone-density result. Lower (more negative) numbers mean weaker bone. Labels and policies use thresholds such as −1.0 or −1.5 [22, 18].
Dysphagia / dysphonia
Difficulty swallowing / voice change, both common short-term effects of anterior neck surgery [27, 58].
Facet joints
Small paired joints at the back of each level that guide motion. Severe facet arthritis is a contraindication to disc replacement [23, 28].
Fluoroscopy
Live X-ray used during surgery [29].
Foramen (plural foramina)
The opening at the side of the spine where a nerve root exits [26].
Foraminotomy (posterior laminoforaminotomy)
An operation through the back of the neck that enlarges the foramen to free a nerve root, usually without fusion [27].
Heterotopic ossification (HO)
Bone forming in soft tissue around an implant. It is graded by the McAfee system (0–IV), and grades III–IV can limit or stop motion [61, 11].
Hybrid construct
A disc replacement at one level and a fusion at another, done in the same operation. Coverage varies [23, 22, 21].
ICER / QALY
Cost-effectiveness measures: the extra cost per extra quality-adjusted life year [24].
IDE (investigational device exemption) trial
An FDA-regulated clinical trial of a device before approval [41].
LCD / NCD
Medicare local / national coverage determination. Cervical disc replacement has an LCD in some regions and no NCD [21].
MAUDE
FDA's database of voluntary adverse-event reports for devices. It is useful for signals, not true rates [70].
MCID (minimal clinically important difference)
The smallest change patients notice as meaningful. Some ADR advantages are statistically but not clinically significant [54].
MR Conditional
Safe in MRI under specified conditions (e.g. 1.5 T and 3 T for Simplify) [18].
Myelopathy
Dysfunction of the spinal cord from compression: clumsy hands, imbalance, weakness [28].
NDI (Neck Disability Index)
A 10-item questionnaire on how neck pain affects daily life, scored 0–50 (or as a percentage) [11].
Odds ratio (OR)
A ratio comparing the odds of an outcome between groups. Below 1 means less likely in the first group (e.g. OR 0.45 for reoperation) [53].
OPLL
Ossification of the posterior longitudinal ligament, a contraindication in NASS criteria [23].
Osteolysis
Bone loss around an implant, often from wear debris and often without symptoms [65, 67].
Osteophyte
A bone spur [26].
PEEK / ZTA
Polyether-ether-ketone (a strong plastic) and zirconia-toughened alumina (a ceramic), used in the Simplify disc [18].
PMA (premarket approval)
FDA's approval pathway for high-risk devices such as artificial discs. Supplements ("S" numbers) add indications such as two levels [13].
Propensity matching / historical control
Statistical matching used when patients were not randomized. Historical controls were treated years earlier [18, 50].
Radiculopathy
A pinched or irritated nerve root causing arm pain, numbness or weakness [26].
SSED
FDA's Summary of Safety and Effectiveness Data for an approved device [18].
Spondylosis
Age-related wear of the discs and joints of the spine, also called arthritis [26].
VAS (visual analog scale)
A 0–100 mm pain line used in trials [11].
