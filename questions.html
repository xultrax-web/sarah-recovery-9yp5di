Questions to Ask Your Surgeon

📚 Deeper reading. This is one of the detailed research pages. You don't need it to get ready. Start with Sarah's home page and What to know.

Medical disclaimer: This private guide is general education compiled from published sources and the public website of Dr. Rovner's practice. It is not medical advice and does not replace the instructions Sarah gets from her surgical team. If anything here differs from what they say, follow them. For trouble breathing or rapidly increasing neck swelling, call 911 (see red-flag card).

Print this page (or the PDF) and bring it to your appointment. Each question links to the part of the guide that explains why it matters.

About my diagnosis

  • Which level(s) are causing my symptoms: C5–C6, C6–C7 or both? What in my exam and MRI makes you sure? Why it matters [32, 33]
  • Do I have radiculopathy, myelopathy, or both? How does that change my options and urgency? [28, 38]
  • Could part of my pain come from my shoulder or a nerve trapped at the elbow or wrist? Do I need an EMG? [26]
  • How likely am I to improve without surgery, and how long is it reasonable to wait? [36, 35]

About disc replacement vs alternatives

  • Am I a candidate for disc replacement? If not, which criterion rules me out (facet arthritis, instability, disc height, bone density)? [23, 18]
  • Would you recommend ACDF or a posterior foraminotomy instead, and why? [27, 59]
  • What results do you expect for my arm pain, neck pain and function? [25, 54]
  • For two levels: are both levels suitable for a disc, or do you suggest a hybrid? [23, 21]

About the device

  • Which device do you plan to use, why that one, and is it FDA-approved for my number of levels? [10, 21]
  • What are its materials, and how will it affect future MRI scans of my neck? [39, 18]
  • Is there any safety alert, recall or osteolysis concern for this device? [20, 70]
  • How will you size it to cover my endplates? (Exposed endplate is linked to heterotopic ossification.) [64]
  • Do you or your practice have financial ties to this device's manufacturer?

About experience

  • How many cervical disc replacements do you do a year? How many two-level?
  • Are you fellowship-trained in spine surgery? (Disc replacement is done by orthopaedic spine surgeons and neurosurgeons.) [29]
  • What is your own rate of reoperation, swallowing problems, and voice problems? [58, 66]
  • Do you perform revisions if a disc needs removal? [65]

About the operation and hospital stay

  • Will this be outpatient (same-day) or will I stay overnight? [25, 18]
  • Which medicines and supplements must I stop, and when? [25, 29]
  • Do I need to stop nicotine, and for how long before and after? [29]
  • Will I need a collar afterward? [25]

About recovery

  • When can I drive, return to my job (desk vs physical), lift, travel, and exercise? [25, 71]
  • When can I return to my sport, and does one vs two levels change that? [72]
  • Will I need physical therapy, and when does it start? [27]
  • Which symptoms after surgery should make me call you, or go to the emergency department? [29]
  • What follow-up X-rays will I have, and for how many years? [29, 20]

About cost and insurance

  • Have you checked my insurer's policy (age limits, PT requirement, DEXA, HbA1c, hybrid coverage)? [22, 21]
  • What will I owe for surgeon, facility, anesthesia and implant? Can I get it in writing? [24]
  • If prior authorization is denied, will your office request a peer-to-peer review or appeal?
