Medical disclaimer: This private guide is general education compiled from published sources and the public website of Dr. Rovner's practice. It is not medical advice and does not replace the instructions Sarah gets from her surgical team. If anything here differs from what they say, follow them. For trouble breathing or rapidly increasing neck swelling, call 911 (see red-flag card).

97 sources were read for this guide (the PDF lists only those cited in the PDF) (full text, abstract, FDA record, or policy text). Numbers in square brackets throughout the site link here. Every source below is cited at least once.

How to read these sources: PubMed abstracts summarize studies; FDA "SSED" documents and device labels are regulatory summaries; insurer policies (Aetna, Medicare LCD) describe coverage, not medical truth; manufacturer documents are marked Industry and should be read with that in mind. Most pivotal disc-replacement trials were sponsored by the device makers.
