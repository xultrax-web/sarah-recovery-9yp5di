A gentle word before Thursday

Feeling nervous is completely normal. One spine practice's patient manual says exactly that, and suggests that anyone treated for anxiety or depression check in with that clinician before surgery [92]. Disc replacement is done through a small incision at the front of the neck, usually in a skin crease, and takes about 2 to 3 hours [25]. Dr. Rovner's practice says many of its patients go home the same day [73].
