Thursday, October 8, 2026You are here

Surgery day

Surgery takes about 2–3 hours [25]. Dr. Rovner's practice says many of its patients go home the same day, and that cervical cases are outpatient or a one-night stay [73, 78]. Someone else drives you home [29, 87]. Stay inside the house for the first 24 hours [87].

Oct 8–10You are here

The first nights

Sleep propped up in a recliner or on pillows [92]. A sore throat and trouble swallowing are common, so sip slowly and use ice chips or popsicles [92, 93]. Most people walk and eat on day 1 [27]. It helps to have someone with you at night at first [92]. Keep the red-flag card handy.

Oct 10–11You are here

Days 2–3: dressing and first shower

The practice says to remove the dressing on day 2 or 3 and leave it off if the wound is dry. Shower once the wound is sealed (about 3 days) and dry, then blot dry and leave the steri-strips alone [76]. Everyday self-care like grooming and simple meals usually resumes around day 2 [29].

Oct 8–14You are here

Week 1

Walk often; the practice discourages bed rest [76]. Avoid "excessive" lifting, bending and twisting [76]. No NSAIDs such as Advil or Aleve [76]. Watch for constipation from pain medicine [76]. Swallowing difficulty may last a week or two [77]. Use the daily log.

Oct 15–21You are here

Week 2

Many people return to desk work, and some to driving, within 1–2 weeks [25, 71]. Don't drive while taking narcotic pain medicine, and ask the surgeon first [91, 93]. No baths, pools or hot tubs until the post-op visit [76]. First follow-up: To fill in: date of first post-op visit.

Oct 22 – Nov 4You are here

Weeks 3–4

Light, regular activities usually resume between 2 and 3 weeks [29]. Swallowing solids and hoarseness can take a few weeks to settle [27]. Call if swallowing is getting worse rather than better [29].

Nov 5–18You are here

Weeks 5–6

Light exercise at 4–6 weeks (expert consensus) [71]. Cleveland Clinic describes full activity except contact sports by 4–6 weeks [29].

Nov 19 – Dec 30You are here

About 6 weeks (Thu, Nov 19) to 12 weeks

An expert panel agreed on more intense conditioning around 6 weeks [71]. Strenuous jobs usually take 6–12 weeks [25]. The practice says that because disc replacement doesn't depend on bone fusing, you can resume activities as tolerated [76]. Ask your surgeon what that means for you.

Around Jan 8, 2027

3 months

Selected athletes return to contact sport around 3 months after one-level surgery (surgeon consensus), with more caution after two levels [72]. Ask about anything high-impact.

Around Apr 8, 2027

6 months

Full recovery can take up to 6 months [29]. Pain and function keep improving over weeks to months, and disc replacement may not remove every bit of pain [25].

Around Oct 8, 2027

1 year and beyond

Follow-up visits check that the disc is working [29]. If your implant is an M6-C, the UK regulator advises yearly X-rays [20].
