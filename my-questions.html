Questions for Your Pre-op Appointment

Medical disclaimer: This private guide is general education compiled from published sources and the public website of Dr. Rovner's practice. It is not medical advice and does not replace the instructions Sarah gets from her surgical team. If anything here differs from what they say, follow them. For trouble breathing or rapidly increasing neck swelling, call 911 (see red-flag card).

Bring this to your pre-op appointment or call. Write the answers right on the printout. You don't need to ask everything. Star the ones that matter most to you.

About your operation

  1. Which level or levels: C5–C6, C6–C7, or both? Why disc replacement rather than fusion for me?
  2. Which implant will you use, and why that one? The practice site mentions the M6 disc, which has since been discontinued (see Which implant?) [77, 20].
  3. Is it possible you'll decide on fusion during surgery instead? If so, when would I find out?
  4. Where will it be done, and will I go home the same day or stay one night [78]?
  5. How long will surgery take? Who will update Wesley, and how?

Before surgery

  1. Which medicines and supplements do I stop, and on what date? When do I restart them [86]?
  2. When exactly do I stop eating and drinking? Are clear liquids allowed, and until when [87]?
  3. What time do I arrive, and who calls me with that time?
  4. Do I need any pre-op tests or medical clearance?

After surgery

  1. What is the after-hours number if something worries me at night?
  2. Which fever should make me call? (Your website says "35 days," which I think means 3–5 days [76].)
  3. Will I need a collar? When can I shower, and when should the dressing come off [76]?
  4. What will I take for pain? Can I use Tylenol, and what's the maximum? No NSAIDs, and for how long [76]?
  5. What are my limits for lifting, bending, and looking down at a phone or laptop?
  6. When can I drive? When can I work, and from home sooner [76, 25]?
  7. When is my first follow-up, and will there be X-rays?
  8. Will I get an implant card, and do I need any long-term imaging for this implant [20]?
