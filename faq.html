Frequently Asked Questions

It is not medical advice and does not replace the instructions Sarah gets from her surgical team. If anything here differs from what they say, follow them. For trouble breathing or rapidly increasing neck swelling, call 911 (see red-flag card).

Short answers. Follow the links for detail and sources.

Is disc replacement better than fusion?

For suitable patients, long-term randomized trials show disc replacement is at least as effective, with fewer repeat operations. Symptom-score differences at 10 years are modest [52, 54, 53]. Fusion remains the standard for many situations where disc replacement isn't suitable [27, 23]. Comparison

Which nerve does a C5–C6 or C6–C7 disc pinch?

Usually the C6 root at C5–C6 and the C7 root at C6–C7 [32]. Symptoms overlap a lot [33]. Anatomy

Will my arm pain go away without surgery?

Most people improve with time and nonsurgical care. In one population study, 90% had no or mild symptoms at follow-up [35, 26]. Conservative care

How long must I try conservative treatment?

Usually at least 6 weeks, unless weakness is severe or progressive or there is myelopathy [23, 21, 22].

Can both C5–C6 and C6–C7 be replaced?

Yes, with a device approved for two contiguous levels: Mobi-C, Prestige LP, Simplify, or prodisc C Vivo/SK [10, 13, 17, 4]. Two-level guide

How long is the surgery and hospital stay?

Usually 2–3 hours. Expect 1–2 days in hospital, or the same day for some people [25].

When can I drive and go back to work?

Driving and desk jobs typically at 1–2 weeks; strenuous jobs at 6–12 weeks [25]. Your surgeon decides for you. Recovery timeline

Will I set off metal detectors or be unable to have MRI?

MRI is often still possible depending on the material; titanium produces the least artifact [39], and Simplify is MR Conditional [18]. Ask about your specific device and carry your implant card. Metal-detector behavior was not covered by the sources reviewed.

How long does an artificial disc last?

Randomized trials have followed patients for up to 10 years with low index-level reoperation rates, e.g. 4.7% at 10 years for two-level Prestige LP [44]. Longer-term wear is still uncertain [56], and some designs have shown osteolysis [67, 20].

What is heterotopic ossification and should I worry?

Extra bone around the implant. It is common, and only high grades seem to affect pain or motion [61, 62]. Details

Am I too old for disc replacement?

There is no single cutoff. Insurers set device-specific age limits, e.g. Aetna covers Mobi-C to 67 and Prestige LP to 78 [22], and bone density matters [18, 21].

Does insurance cover it?

Often yes, if criteria are met. Medicare coverage depends on your regional contractor, and commercial policies vary [21, 22]. Costs & insurance

Can I play contact sports afterward?

Expert surgeons disagree. Most-endorsed return to games was 3 months after one-level surgery, and there was much less support after two levels [72].

I have an M6-C. What should I do?

The UK MHRA recommends annual X-rays to monitor for osteolysis, even without symptoms [20]. Talk to your surgeon about a follow-up plan.

Does smoking rule me out?

Not necessarily: one study found similar outcomes in smokers [40]. Surgeons generally advise stopping nicotine because it impairs healing [29, 25].
